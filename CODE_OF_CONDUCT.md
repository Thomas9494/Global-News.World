# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Global News to be a project anyone can contribute to — whatever your country,
language, politics, background, experience, or whether you write code at all.

Everyone taking part pledges to make participation a harassment-free experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good faith. Contributors write in many languages and not all of them in their
  first — read for meaning, not for tone.
- Focus on what is best for readers of the map.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Specific to this project

This project catalogues news outlets from countries in conflict with one another,
including state broadcasters, partisan press and outlets that consider each other
propaganda. That is deliberate: seeing them side by side is the point of the map.

- **Arguing about whether a source belongs in the catalog is legitimate and welcome.**
  Make the case on the merits — who runs it, what it publishes, whether it is a real
  newsroom.
- **Using the project as a venue for the underlying political conflict is not.** Do not
  attack contributors for the country they are from or the outlets they submit.
- Editing the catalog to suppress a country's or a side's press, or padding it to
  amplify one, will be reverted and is grounds for a ban.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take
appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem
inappropriate. They may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a confidential issue or contacting a maintainer
directly. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The
privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.

### Consequences

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning explaining what was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — no interaction with the people involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban** — from any interaction with the project for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban** — from any public interaction with the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions — and when an individual is representing the project in public.
